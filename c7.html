Course: Business and Entrepreneurship


Overview:

This course is designed for aspiring entrepreneurs who want to start and manage a successful business. You’ll learn the fundamentals of business planning, management, finance, and growth strategies.


What You’ll Learn:

1. Business Fundamentals

Understanding business models and types
Market research and competitive analysis
Setting goals and business planning

2. Startup Essentials

How to register and structure a business
Funding options and managing finances
Legal and compliance basics

3. Management & Operations

Team building and leadership
Operations and workflow optimization
Customer service and client relations

4. Growth & Marketing

Branding and marketing strategies
Sales and business development techniques
Scaling your business sustainably


Who this course is for:

Aspiring entrepreneurs and business owners
Students and professionals wanting to learn business skills
Anyone interested in turning ideas into a profitable business


Outcome:

Develop a business plan and strategy
Manage operations and finances effectively
Learn the essentials for launching and growing a successful business


Duration:

Typically 5–7 weeks

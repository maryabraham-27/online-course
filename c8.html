Course: Artificial Intelligence Fundamentals


Overview:

This course introduces the foundational concepts of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML). You’ll learn how AI works, explore practical applications, and understand its impact on modern technology.


What You’ll Learn:

1. Introduction to AI

History and evolution of AI
Key AI concepts and terminology
Understanding intelligent systems

2. Machine Learning Basics

Supervised vs. unsupervised learning
Regression, classification, and clustering
Data preprocessing and model evaluation

3. AI Tools and Technologies

Overview of AI frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch, etc.)
Introduction to natural language processing (NLP) and computer vision
AI in real-world applications

4. Practical Applications

AI in business, healthcare, and education
Automation and predictive analytics
Ethical considerations and AI governance


Who this course is for:

Beginners interested in AI and ML
Students or professionals exploring technology careers
Anyone curious about AI applications in the real world


Outcome:

Understand the basics of AI and ML
Gain practical knowledge of AI tools and techniques
Apply AI concepts to solve simple real-world problems


Duration:

Typically 6–8 weeks

Course: Digital Marketing Fundamentals


Overview:

This course introduces the essential concepts of digital marketing, helping you understand how businesses promote themselves online. You’ll learn practical strategies to increase visibility, engage audiences, and drive results.


What You’ll Learn:

1. SEO (Search Engine Optimization)

Understanding search engines
Keyword research and optimization
On-page and off-page SEO techniques

2. Social Media Marketing

Platforms like Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and Twitter
Content creation and engagement strategies
Paid advertising basics

3. Online Advertising

Google Ads fundamentals
Display and video advertising
Campaign tracking and analytics

4. Digital Marketing Strategy

Creating marketing campaigns
Measuring success using analytics
Optimizing campaigns for better ROI


Who this course is for:

Beginners interested in digital marketing
Small business owners or entrepreneurs
Anyone looking to start a career in marketing


Outcome:

Understand key digital marketing channels
Create basic campaigns and strategies
Improve website visibility and engagement online


Duration:

Typically 4–6 weeks

Course: Graphic Design Essentials


Overview:

This course introduces learners to the fundamentals of graphic design. You’ll learn key design principles and get hands-on experience with popular tools like Photoshop and Illustrator to create visually appealing graphics.


What You’ll Learn:

1. Design Principles

Color theory, typography, and layout
Composition, balance, and contrast
Visual hierarchy and branding basics

2. Graphic Design Tools

Adobe Photoshop: image editing, retouching, and digital artwork
Adobe Illustrator: vector graphics, logos, and illustrations

3. Practical Projects

Logo design
Social media graphics
Poster and flyer creation

4. Creative Workflow

From concept to final design
Tips for efficient design practices
Preparing files for web and print


Who this course is for:

Beginners in graphic design
Entrepreneurs or marketers wanting to create visual content
Anyone looking to improve creative and design skills


Outcome:

Understand core graphic design concepts
Create professional-quality visuals using Photoshop and Illustrator
Build a small portfolio of design projects


Duration:

Typically 4–6 weeks

Course: Cybersecurity and Ethical Hacking


Overview:

This course introduces the fundamentals of cybersecurity and ethical hacking. You’ll learn how to protect networks, systems, and data from cyber threats while understanding the ethical and legal aspects of security testing.


What You’ll Learn:

1. Introduction to Cybersecurity

Cyber threats and types of attacks
Security principles and best practices
Network and system security basics

2. Ethical Hacking Fundamentals

Penetration testing methodology
Vulnerability assessment
Tools used by ethical hackers

3. Data Protection & Privacy

Encryption and cryptography basics
Secure handling of sensitive data
Compliance with legal and regulatory standards

4. Practical Skills

Setting up test environments
Simulating attacks and defenses
Reporting and documenting findings


Who this course is for:

Beginners interested in cybersecurity
IT professionals who want to expand their knowledge
Anyone looking to pursue a career in ethical hacking or information security


Outcome:

Understand the fundamentals of online security
Perform basic ethical hacking tests
Apply data protection techniques to secure systems


Duration:

Typically 6–8 weeks
